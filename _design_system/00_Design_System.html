Design System

One look for every Interview Prep page. Tokens live in _shared/design-tokens.css — change a value there, never inside a page.

1Colour palette

Category colours — each section / subject uses one

Every colour has 4 shades: border (main), bg light fill for the section, dk dark for titles/text, lt soft line for inner boxes. Inside a coloured section, boxes are white with the lt border.

6Page layout

Site structure

Nav bar on every page: brand left · subject centre · progress / toggles right. No breadcrumbs.

7Guidelines

Fill spare height with padding and gaps (the --k multiplier).
Zoom or enlarge fonts to fill space.
Use the subject's category colour for the section; white boxes inside.
Plain white slides, or more than one strong colour in a box.
Show it working: steppers, simulators, quizzes with "▶ Try it".
Long paragraphs or internal JDK/framework source code.
Examples from FireFlink work (notifications, Kafka events, license DBs) — marked "simplified".
Claim illustrative code is the real code.
Arrows end at box edges; labels never overlap shapes; text wraps inside its box.
Fixed-width boxes that let words spill out at smaller screens.
Check every slide at 1365×620: no overflow, no clipped text, no JS errors.
Deliver without looking at screenshots of the slides you changed.
